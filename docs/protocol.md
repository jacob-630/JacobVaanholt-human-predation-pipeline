# Systematic Review: Human Predation on Vertebrates

## Protocol Version: V1.0
## Date: October 9, 2026

### 1. Scientific Question
How successful are humans at capturing wild vertebrate prey (fish, mammals, birds, reptiles) across different hunting methods and stages of the predation sequence?

### 2. Scope
- **Taxa:** All wild vertebrates (Ungulates, Fish, Birds, Reptiles, Amphibians).
- **Geography:** North America (Canada + US).
- **Timeframe:** 1980 – 2026.

### 3. AI Workflow Strategy
- **Search:** Automated API queries (Scopus/WoS).
- **Screening:** ASReview (Active Learning) for efficiency.
- **Extraction:** LangChain/CrewAI Agents with "Human-in-the-loop" verification.
